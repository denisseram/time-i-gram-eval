assumptions:
  - ISO year and week parsed from Kalenderwoche (YYYY-Www), not from Saison
  - "winter" for the caption statistic = ISO weeks 40-12; "early-year wave" = mean of weeks 1-10
  - "Complete" years = years with at least 52 reported weeks (2012 and 2026 are partial)
limitations:
  - ggtime 1.1.0 bug: coord_calendar fails on a yearweek axis (looks for a "month" granule); workaround is a Date axis with explicit cal_isoweek granules
  - Year labels are ggtime row labels pushed into the left margin, not true y-axis labels (the y axis has no meaning in coord_calendar)
  - Week-axis labels are the break positions (1..53) of the reference row, because ggtime's ISO-week labels show week 1 of the next year in the 53rd slot
  - Week 52 is low in most years, which probably reflects reporting over the Christmas holidays, not lower illness
