assumptions:

  - A month counts as missing only if it has zero daily rows; a partly covered month still shows a value
  - The caption's temperature range = warmest minus coldest calendar month, each averaged over all years
limitations:
  - Partly covered months may be biased toward the days that do have readings; the number of days behind each cell is shown on hover
  - The colour scale runs from the lowest to the highest monthly mean in the data, so small differences between neighbouring cells are hard to read exactly
