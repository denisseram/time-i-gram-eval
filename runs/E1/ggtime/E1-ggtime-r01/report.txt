deliverable: plot.R
requirements:
  R1: done - mean of daily means per depth × month, 7 depth rows in a heatmap
  R2: done - Feb 2018 to Dec 2025, one tile per month, ggtime scale_x_mixtime with yearly ticks
  R3: done - tsibble fill_gaps(.full = TRUE) adds explicit NA months, drawn as blank cells
  R4: done - y-axis labels "2 m" to "40 m" in depth order, shallowest at top
  R5: done - colour legend titled "Monthly mean temperature (°C)"
  R6: done - title plus a one-sentence caption built from data (calendar-month averages at 2 m and 40 m)
assumptions:
  - Monthly mean = unweighted mean of the daily means (not weighted by n_obs)
  - "Missing" = a month with no daily row for that depth; a month with even one day of data is shown
  - The caption's "winter"/"peak" values are calendar-month averages over all years, as a typical-year summary
  - Used mixtime::yearmonth for the x variable because scale_x_mixtime fails on tsibble's yearmonth class
limitations:
  - Months with only a few days of data look as reliable as full months; data coverage is not shown
  - The year tick labels sit near the middle of each year, not at the January boundary
  - A colour heatmap shows small differences between depths less precisely than lines would
