# Rubric E1: monthly water temperature per depth

| Req | Met (2) when | Partial (1) if |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | 7 series (2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40 m) of monthly means | daily values shown instead of monthly means; a depth missing |
| R2 | 2018-02 to 2025-12, one position per month | range cut; uneven month spacing |
| R3 | months without data are gaps/empty | gaps bridged by a line, or drawn as 0 |
| R4 | depth identifiable, ordered 2 → 40 m | legend in arbitrary (e.g. lexical 10, 15, 2, …) order |
| R5 | °C on axis or colour legend | unit missing |
| R6 | title + one-sentence caption that matches the data | caption claims something the data does not show |

## Ground-truth checks

- Months with **no data** (must be gaps):
  - 2 m: 2019-08, 2019-09, 2019-10, 2024-06 … 2024-10
  - 5 m: 2023-08, 2023-09, 2023-10, 2024-10, 2025-12
  - 40 m: 2022-12 … 2023-04
  - 10, 15, 20, 30 m: none
- Highest monthly mean: 2 m 19.3 °C (2025-09); 10 m 18.6 °C (2025-09); 40 m 10.9 °C (2022-10).
- Pattern: summer stratification (surface far warmer than 40 m, Aug–Oct), winter mixing (all depths 2–4 °C, Feb–Mar).
- Lexical sort trap: depth is an integer; a string sort gives 10, 15, 2, 20, …
