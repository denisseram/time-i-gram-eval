| Req | Met (2) when | Partial (1) if |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | 15 rows, 2012 to 2026, chronological top to bottom | calendar years instead of ISO years; wrong order |
| R2 | one cell per ISO week, week 53 present in 2015 and 2020 | week 53 dropped or merged; weeks placed by date not ISO week (shifted cells around New Year) |
| R3 | sequential colour + legend with unit | unit missing; diverging or categorical scale |
| R4 | 2012-W01…W39 and 2026-W44…W53 empty | filled as 0 or omitted rows |
| R5 | week labels on x, year labels on y | one of them missing |
| R6 | title + caption that matches the data | caption contradicts data |

## Ground-truth checks

- Filter: `Bundesland == "Bundesweit"`, `Altersgruppe == "00+"` gives **734 weeks**, 2012-W40 to 2026-W43, no gaps.
  Forgetting the filter mixes 17 regions × 6 age groups (24,756 rows) into each cell.
- Weeks per row: 2012 → 13 (W40–W52), 2015 → 53, 2020 → 53, 2026 → 43, all others 52.
- Maximum: 3,215 in 2022-W50.
- Visible patterns: low summer (about 300–1,000), high Dec–Mar; a sharp dip in week 52 every year
  (for example 2018: W51 = 1,344, W52 = 463, W01 = 901); pandemic-shaped 2020–2021.
- Parsing: `Kalenderwoche` is `YYYY-Www`. Week 1 of an ISO year can start in late December (e.g. 2020-W01
  starts 2019-12-30); placing cells by calendar date instead of ISO week misplaces such weeks.
