# Task E6: A spiral of acute respiratory illness, one turn per year

Make one visualization with {{TOOL_NAME}} that shows the weekly series as a **spiral with one turn per
year**, so that the yearly cycles can be compared turn by turn.

## Data

- URL: {{DATA_URL}}
- Format: **tab-separated**, one header row.
- Publisher: Robert Koch-Institut (RKI), ARE-Praxis-Sentinel. License: CC BY 4.0.
- Weekly estimates of how many people per 100,000 consulted a doctor's practice for a new acute
  respiratory illness (ARE). Columns:

  | Column | Type | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|
  | `Saison` | text (`YYYY/YY`) | Season: ISO week 40 of the first year to ISO week 39 of the next |
  | `Kalenderwoche` | text (`YYYY-Www`) | Reporting week in ISO 8601 week format |
  | `Bundesland` | text | `Bundesweit` (all of Germany) or one of the 16 states |
  | `Bundesland_ID` | integer | `0` for `Bundesweit`, 1–16 for the states |
  | `Altersgruppe` | text | Age group: `00+` (all ages), `0-4`, `5-14`, `15-34`, `35-59`, `60+` |
  | `ARE_Konsultationsinzidenz` | integer | ARE consultations per 100,000 population in that week |

- **Use only the rows with `Bundesland` = `Bundesweit` and `Altersgruppe` = `00+`.**

## Requirements

- **R1.** A **spiral**: the angle is the ISO week of the year, **ISO week 1 at the top**, clockwise, one turn
  per ISO year; the distance from the centre grows with time, so later years are further out.
- **R2.** `ARE_Konsultationsinzidenz` is encoded on the spiral (for example colour or line thickness), with
  a legend stating the unit (consultations per 100,000 per week).
- **R3.** Each turn is labelled with its year.
- **R4.** Week numbers (or months) are marked around the spiral.
- **R5.** The figure has a title and a one-sentence caption stating the main pattern you see.
